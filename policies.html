Policies

Clear expectations

Straightforward policies so you know what to expect before, during, and after service.

Deposits

A $5 deposit reserves the appointment.

Cancellations

Deposits are nonrefundable for:

  • Missed appointments
  • Cancellations within 24 hours
  • Failure to provide access or required materials

Payment

Payment is due after service unless otherwise stated in a contract or invoice.

Hardware and Software

Hardware, licenses, subscriptions, domains, hosting, and third-party services are billed separately unless included in writing.

Data Responsibility

Customers should maintain backups before service begins. GK Solutions will use reasonable care but cannot guarantee recovery or prevent every possible data-loss event.

Credentials

Customers should avoid sharing passwords whenever possible. Temporary credentials should be changed after service.

Estimates

Estimates may change if the actual scope differs from the information originally provided. Customers must approve additional work before it proceeds.

Media Retention

Converted files remain available for 30 days when delivered through cloud storage. Working copies are deleted afterward.

Project Ownership

Website and software ownership, licensing, hosting, maintenance, source code, and third-party components should be defined in the project agreement.
