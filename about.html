About

Curiosity, built in

GK Solutions was created after nearly a decade of working across IT, software, support, systems, and technical problem-solving.

The company exists to make those skills available to individuals, nonprofits, businesses, and organizations without making technology feel inaccessible or unnecessarily complicated.

GK Solutions takes a practical approach: understand the problem, ask the right questions, and build or recommend something that genuinely fits.
