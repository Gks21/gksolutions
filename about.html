About

Built from curiosity.

I've spent nearly a decade working across different areas of IT, from technical support and systems to software and web development.

GK Solutions came from wanting to take that broader experience and put it to use in new ways.

I enjoy figuring out how things work, why they don't work, and whether there's a better way to do them. Sometimes that means fixing a computer. Sometimes it means planning a network, building a website, developing software, or simply helping someone make a better technology decision.

GK Solutions gives me the opportunity to do all of those things while working directly with the people and organizations using them.

Every good solution starts with curiosity. Ours is built in.
