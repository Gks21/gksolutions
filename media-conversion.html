Media Conversion

Preserve your memories

Convert VHS tapes and home-recorded DVDs into accessible digital files with local pickup and return.

DVD

$10 per disc containing up to one recorded hour. Add $5 for each additional recorded hour. Bulk collections may receive package pricing.

Process

  1. Schedule pickupCustomer schedules a pickup appointment.
  2. CollectionGK Solutions collects the media.
  3. LabelingEach item is labeled and documented.
  4. ConversionMedia is converted into a digital format.
  5. VerificationFiles receive basic technical verification.
  6. ReturnOriginal media is returned.
  7. InvoiceCustomer receives an itemized invoice and receipt.
  8. DeliveryDigital files are delivered physically or through a secure folder.

Delivery options

  • Customer-provided USB drive
  • Storage purchased through GK Solutions
  • Free secure cloud delivery

Cloud folders remain available for 30 days. Customers are responsible for downloading and saving their files before expiration.

Privacy

GK Solutions does not watch, review, or catalog personal footage. Files are accessed only as necessary to complete the conversion and verify basic technical quality. Temporary working files are deleted after the stated retention period.

Limitations

GK Solutions does not accept:

  • Damaged tapes
  • Moldy tapes
  • Media requiring repair
  • Commercial copyrighted DVDs
  • Content that cannot legally be copied
  • Cleanup or restoration requests
